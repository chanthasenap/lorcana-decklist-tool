Peter's Decklist Sharing Tool

Paste a Dreamborn.ink deck link and get one shareable image — card art, prices, and total deck cost — plus cost curve, colors, and inkable stats as text, ready to drop straight into Discord, or post directly to a channel with prices still clickable.

  1. Paste your deck link. Open your deck on Dreamborn.ink, copy the URL from your browser (e.g. https://dreamborn.ink/decks/…), and paste it below.
  2. Click Generate. This runs entirely in your browser — it reads your deck straight from Dreamborn's public data, no account or upload needed.
  3. Share it. Once it's ready, the Preview tab shows the full image and stats, and the Share tab posts it straight to a Discord channel with prices still clickable.

Or import from duels.ink

duels.ink doesn't allow linking a deck directly into another site, so open your deck there, click Export → Simple to copy it, and paste the result below instead — it gets matched against the same card database.

Or scan physical cards

Best results: lay cards face-up and right-side up, spaced apart with no overlap, on a plain dark surface with even lighting. Take a few photos to cover the whole deck — recognized cards add up across photos, and you'll get a chance to fix any misreads before generating.

Card recognition reads the printed name on each card via on-device text recognition — it can misread art-heavy or angled cards, so always double-check the review list below before generating.

Review matched cards

Fix any wrong name or quantity, remove anything that shouldn't be there, or add a card manually — nothing gets generated until you confirm.

Card thumbnails and prices above are clickable — they open the card's TCGplayer listing. The deck title/link opens the deck on Dreamborn.ink.

Post the downloaded image and paste this link as text in the same Discord message — that's the one-click path to the deck for your team. Or skip this and use the Share tab to post directly with live links.

Share directly to Discord

Posts a message to one specific channel — card art plus a fully clickable price list and deck link, not a flat image.

One-time setup: in Discord, open the channel you want to post to → Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL, then paste it above. Each webhook is tied to one specific channel, so this is how you pick where it posts. The URL is saved only in your own browser (never sent anywhere but Discord) so you only have to paste it once.
